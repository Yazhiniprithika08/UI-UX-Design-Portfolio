Case Study

AI Lead Qualification Agent

B2B SaaS Platform

Executive Summary

Problem Statement

B2B sales teams spend upwards of 60% of their time dialing and emailing unqualified leads, which severely stalls sales velocity and inflates acquisition costs. Manual lead scoring is subjective, slow, and prone to error.

Objectives

  • Accelerate initial response time from hours to under 2 minutes.
  • Automate profiling and contextual validation utilizing semantic prompts.
  • Equip sales reps with high-fidelity analytics and context logs.

Target Users & Persona

SJ
Sarah Jenkins

Sales Operations Manager

"I need an automated, transparent way to filter low-intent signups so my reps focus only on high-value accounts."
